# Rise of Seamoor 🏰⚔️

> **A 3D Medieval City Builder & Strategy Game**

**Rise of Seamoor** allows players to take on the role of Ares, the lord and hero of the seaside village of Seamoor. Combining classic city-building mechanics with 2D platforming campaigns and 3D RTS-style battles, the game challenges you to transform a humble village into a powerful historical landmark.

## 🧐 About the Game

**Rise of Seamoor** is a genre-blending strategy game developed in **Unity**. It uniquely mixes three distinct gameplay styles:

1.  **3D City Building:** Manage resources, construct buildings, and expand your territory.
2.  **2D Campaign:** A side-scrolling adventure mode to gather extra resources and progress the narrative.
3.  **RTS Battles:** Tactical tower defense combat where you must defend your city using troops produced during the building phase.

*Developed as part of the Video Game Development curricular unit in the Degree in Informatics Engineering.*

## 📜 Story

Ares, abandoned by his family at a young age, was taken in by the small seaside town of **Seamoor**. Growing up to be a fighter known for his charisma and selflessness, Ares is determined to repay the village that raised him.

Now, as the Lord of Seamoor, his goal is to dedicate his life to making this village one of the most important and powerful locations in history.

## 🎮 Key Features & Modes

### 🏙️ 3D Mode: City Builder
* **Construct & Manage:** Build structures to grow your economy.
* **Production:** Manage resource production and train troops.
* **Evolution:** Upgrade your Town Hall to unlock new eras and capabilities.

### 🗡️ 2D Mode: The Campaign
* **Adventure:** A side-scrolling platformer mode where you control Ares directly.
* **Resource Gathering:** Complete levels to obtain resources needed for faster city evolution.
* **Lore:** Discover more about the hero's backstory.

### ⚔️ Battle Mode: RTS Defense
* **Defend Seamoor:** Occurs every time you evolve the Town Hall.
* **Tactics:** Position the troops you produced in City Builder mode.
* **Strategy:** A top-down 3D view where you must repel invaders to secure your city's growth.

## ⚙️ Installation

The game is currently available for **Windows**.

1.  Download the latest build from [Itch.io](https://pmoura.itch.io/rise-of-seamoor).
2.  Unzip the `Rise of Seamoor.zip` file.
3.  Run the executable (`.exe`) file to launch the game.

**Inputs:** Keyboard and Mouse.
